## Introduction

The story of SuperTux focuses on Tux the penguin who has to save his beloved girlfriend Penny
from the clutches of the evil Nolok.

## Icy Island

### Intro: Picnic With Penny

(Plays at the start of the game - see also [Milestone 1 version](https://github.com/SuperTux/supertux/blob/v0.1.4/data/intro.txt))

*" Somewhere at the shores of Antarctica... "*

Tux the penguin was going to meet his friend, Penny, for a picnic. They were having quite a good time, when suddenly,
an ice bush started shaking. However neither Tux nor Penny noticed. All of a sudden, Tux saw a creature jump out from
the ice bush, there was a flash, and Tux fell unconscious.

Tux woke up, dizzy, to find that Penny was missing! Tux was starting to become worried. He then saw a letter, and it read:

*" Tux, I have kidnapped your beloved Penny and have taken her to my fortress.\
The path to my fortress is littered with my minions.\
You don't have a chance of saving her!\
    Signed, Nolok*

Realizing the trouble Penny could be in, Tux became determined to save her. Them, Tux saw Nolok's castle in the distance,
and set off to rescue Penny!

![](images/Introcutscene.png)

### Interlude: An Unsuspected Follower

(Plays after finishing the level "The End of the Tunnel")

After an entire day of navigating Icy Island, Tux had now almost reached the supposed castle of Nolok. The lack of sleep
during the night may not have been ideal, but he was determined to keep going! Not wanting to waste any more time, Tux
continued on, sliding down the hill he was on into the icy valley up ahead.

Unbeknownst to Tux, a Yeti seemed very keen on his presence as he kept a close eye on Tux as he continued his journey.
The yeti let out a loud roar and proceeded to follow the little penguin.

### Interlude: Nobody Home

(Plays at the end of the level "Castle of Nolok")

At last, Tux managed to rush towards the final room of the icy castle. To his surprise, there was no one here. Neither
Penny, nor this... strange Nolok guy. Surely they had to be here somewhere, right? Perhaps a hidden room?

After a bit of back and forth with himself, Tux noticed a small note attached to one of the pillars. It was yet another
note, by that Nolok. The note read that he had already left Icy Island towards the far north. If Tux wished to follow him
and save his beloved friend, he would have to come to the Crystal Mine. Marked below was a rough map of the Glacier Isle,
showing a boat and some kind of small pier on the other end.

But before Tux could even make sense of any of this, a loud roar filled the room! The mighty Yeti had made his appearance,
busting his chest in an intimidating fashion! Tux made his way towards a balcony on the other end of the room. With no other
option, he jumped!

Unfortunately, the Yeti would not let him go so easily...

![](images/Interlude01.jpg)

### Interlude: Off to the Glacier Isles

(Plays after beating the Yeti)

He did it! He actually did it! Tux had beaten the mighty yeti! While it did not take long for the yeti to get up
again, to Tux's relief, he did not throw hands again. The yeti was furious however! He could not understand how
a tiny penguin like Tux could have beaten him in a fight. Almost as if he forgot Tux was there to begin with,
the yeti rushed off, grumbling to himself. Not sure what to make of it, Tux decided to continue moving onwards.

As he reached the shore, Tux could already see the Glacier Isle on the horizon. If he knew Nolok went over there
sooner, he could have been there much faster. Perhaps that was Nolok's goal with the note he left behind...

### Outro: The Crystal Mine

(Plays upon reaching Glacier Isle)

After a long swim across the antarctic waters, Tux arrived at the Glacier Isle mentioned in Nolok's note.
A massive shaft lay before him, leading down into the aforementioned Crystal Mine. Still carrying a spark
of doubt that this was not a trap, Tux slowly descended into the depths once more...


At last, Tux made it through the crystal mine and found himself on the opposite end of the Glacier Isle.
As he was about to exit the mine, Tux was internally hoping that all Nolok wrote in the note was true.
To his relief he did find a boat still docked. With it, he could sail up north, to find Nolok and save Penny.
Tux went on the boat and began the long journey up north.

It seemed the map in the note wasn't a trap after all.\
The more he thought about it, the less Tux was sure about Nolok's motivations. Why would he leave all these clues behind?

## Rooted Forest

### Intro: A New Location

(Plays immediately after setting foot on world 2)

After quite a long trip, Tux arrived at the new biome. Tux was astonished by the scale of this colorful land which was quite
different from his home. But how was he ever going to find Nolok in this vast forest?

Tux thought for a moment and decided to find a place high up to get a better view. Maybe he could find some
kind of landmark to help him locate Nolok, wherever he may be.

### Interlude: From Afar It Spreads

(Plays right before the corrupted forest)

Never had Tux been missing sunlight more than before. Since the rain had stopped and the sky was clear,
Tux hoped to have found a nice, safe place to take a short break.

Just as he was about to rest, the sudden sound of rustling leaves from a bush nearby startled Tux. He went to
investigate and got startled by a Vicious Ivy, tumbling out of the bushes! However, this one looked different.
Its usual bright green leaves seemed to have turned an ailing purple.

Before Tux even had a chance to think about what had happened to the poor thing, the ground began to shake
violently...

Massive purple roots burst through the ground, followed by an onslaught of many more coming closer and closer!
Tux panicked! Whatever these roots were, they were covering everything. Where could he go?

But he couldn't give up now! Tux could not leave her behind. Not in a place being overrun by these violent
roots.\
He must rescue Penny! He must get to that tower!

![](images/Ghostforest.png)

### Interlude: The Root of The Problem

(Plays at the end of the tower level of Rooted Forest)

High on the very top of the dilapidated tower, once again Tux found... nothing. He could not believe it.
He was shocked. Confused. Was this not where he needed to go? Did Nolok's hints trick him after all? Did he
walk into a trap??

Tux had not long to think about all this, because the tower began shaking violently. His assumption prove
correct instantly.

He made it! He escaped the collapsing tower just in time as the structure now fully gave in. But the roots
were not done yet!\
This was only the beginning, as he had found the root of the problem, towering over the small penguin...

### Interlude: Nature is Healing

(Played after beating the Ghost Tree)

Was it over? Was the nightmare finally over?? Tux could not believe his eyes, but it seemed so! The roots began
retracting. The ground looked more vital again.

Though as much as he was ecstatic about seeing the land restored and beginning to heal... where to now?\
The forest, the tower... those were the only leads Tux had. He had no clue where Nolok could be hiding, if
he even was here to begin with. Or rather, any more.

There was no one around Tux could ask for help. All but the remains of corrupted husks littered the ground
as he moved along in thought. No living creature in sight.\
Hoping to discover some kind of clue - anything! - Tux's best bet was to continue exploring this side of the
forest, as destroyed as it might be.

There must be something, somewhere, ably to help him to find Nolok! Right...?

### Outro: Floral Blossom

Despite the Ghost Tree's defeat and a sliver of life returning to the once lush forest, the land was still in
shambles. The ground was riddled with cracks and crevices, and now one of them was blocking Tux's path...\
Fortunately for him, a Granito, alive and well, revealed himself, excited to see Tux on the other side of the
crevice.

The Granito spoke of three groups of his fellow Granito who had gotten lost while trying to escape the roots
near the east coast. They had been swallowed by the earth as the ground collapsed in on itself. If Tux could
help find them, the could help him reach the other side.

Tux agreed to help! Soon afterwards, he noticed a decaying root revealing a path leading underground, so he
began his search for the lost Granito.


With the help of the saved Granito, Tux managed to reach the other side. He was glad that there were more
Granito that had survived the Ghost Tree's corruption.

One of the Granito asked Tux what a penguin like himself was doing so far from the Antarctic. Tux replied
that he was looking for his friend Penny, and that she was captured by an evil creature named *"Nolok"* –
a name the Granito remembered unpleasantly...

The Granito mentioned they knew Nolok's homeland, but whether he still called it his home or if he held
Penny there, they did not know for certain. The Granito offered to show Tux the way. It was the least
they could do to show their gratitude.

Tux gladly accepted their offer. Together with a handful of Granito, he soon began his journey to the
tropics, in the northeast.

The remaining Granito would stay behind, slowly rebuilding their home, now that the corruption had finally
ceased.

## Nolok's Motive

Once Tux reaches the end of Rooted Forest, Nolok starts to notice Tux's determination being impressed by his commitment
and bravery to push through even leaving his homeland. After defeating Nolok and rescuing Penny, Nolok acknowledges Tux
and Penny's bravery shown a more honorable side. He claims to not change his ways due to his nature as a predator, but
that he respects the two as his equals.
